# ADR-013: Nigeria as launch market

Status: Proposed

## Context

The PRD used Canada as an example. The team and early users are in Lagos.

## Decision

Launch in Nigeria first. The schema stores ISO country codes so more markets can follow.

## Alternatives considered

Canada; a multi-market launch.

## Consequences

Decides the identity verification provider, privacy law (NDPA), phone and currency formats. Awaiting client sign-off.
